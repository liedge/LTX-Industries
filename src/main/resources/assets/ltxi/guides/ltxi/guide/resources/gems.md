# Gems and Minerals

## Olivine

<ItemGrid>
<ItemIcon id="raw_olivine" />
<ItemIcon id="olivine" />
</ItemGrid>

Olivine is a general purpose gem used for laser devices for machines, tools, and weapons. When energized it forms efficient
digital cutting edges and - if given enough energy - can crystallize light into solid fragments.
Raw Olivine can be found by processing [Peridotite](../building_blocks/peridotite.md) Dust through the [HydroSieve](../machines/processing/hydrosieve.md).
This process has a low yield, it is recommended to set up an automated passive line as soon as possible to overcome
early machine low speeds.

## Fluorite

<ItemGrid>
<ItemIcon id="raw_fluorite" />
<ItemIcon id="fluorite" />
</ItemGrid>

Fluorite contains high natural concentrations of Fluorine, extractable by dissolving the crystals in Sulfuric Acid to form
[Hydrofluoric Acid](acids.md#hydrofluoric-acid). Raw Fluorite can be found by processing Deepslate Dust through the HydroSieve.
Like Olivine, a dedicated setup is recommended as Hydrofluoric Acid is vital for producing the advanced Fluoropolymer.

## Pyroxene

<ItemImage id="pyroxene" />

Pyroxene is a special gem recovered from Raw Olivine through the [third stage](../ore_processing.md#stage-3) of
the ore processing line. The unique structure of this crystal can form nano-processor logic cores used for the most
advanced [circuits](tech_parts.md#elite-circuitry).
