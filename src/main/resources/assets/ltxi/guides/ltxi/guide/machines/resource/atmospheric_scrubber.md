# Atmospheric Scrubber

<BlockImage id="atmospheric_scrubber" scale="4" />

## About

The Atmospheric Scrubber specializes in extracting gases and fluids from the environment. It can choose to extract ambient
resources from the dimension, or it can extract localized resources from specific locations such as the Overworld's oceans
or swamps.

## Specs

- Base cycle time: 30 seconds
- No inputs
- Outputs: 1-4 fluids, 0-2 items
