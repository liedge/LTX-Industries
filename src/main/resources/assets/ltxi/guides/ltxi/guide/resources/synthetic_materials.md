# Synthetic Resources

## Sulfuric Acid

<ItemImage id="sulfuric_acid_bucket" />

Sulfuric Acid is the first compound chemical available, made from mixing acidic biomass into water or reacting
Sulphurine with water. It is the base for polymer production, Hydrofluoric Acid production, the fourth stage of
ore processing, and dissolution of exotic flora.
