# Silicone Compounds

## Silicone Oil

<ItemImage id="portable_tank" components="limacore:fluid={id:'ltxi:silicone_oil', amount:16000}" scale="2" />

Silicone Oil is a stable, non-toxic lubricant made from [Silicon](elements_base.md#silicon), [Methane](elements_compound.md#methane),
and [Chlorine](elements_base.md#chlorine) reacting together in the [Chem Lab](../machines/processing/chem_lab.md). This
produces Silicone Oil and some Hydrochloric Acid as a waste product. It is currently only used as a precursor for Silicone Rubber.

## Silicone Rubber

<ItemImage id="silicone_rubber" />

Introducing [Sulphurine](elements_compound.md#sulphurine) to Silicone Oil in a Chem Lab will cause it to rapidly
catalyze into Silicone Rubber. This material is used to craft high capacity storage upgrades, high-energy tech parts,
and the lining of the [Wonderland](../wonderland.md) armor set.
