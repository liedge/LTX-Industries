## Basic Circuitry

<ItemGrid>
<ItemIcon id="polymer_sheet" />
<ItemIcon id="copper_plate" />
<ItemIcon id="circuit_board" />
<ItemIcon id="silicon_plate" />
<ItemIcon id="t1_circuit" />
<ItemIcon id="t2_circuit" />
<ItemIcon id="t3_circuit" />
</ItemGrid>

Etching copper plates onto polymer sheets with [Sulfuric Acid](acids.md#sulfuric-acid) produces circuit boards suitable
for entry to mid-range electronics. Silicon plates, conductive metal plates, titanium shielding plates, and an appropriate
voltaic cell(s) are enough to manufacture up to the third tier of circuitry. Because of material limitations, circuits
using this method must be built on top of another. Still, it is more efficient than doing so by hand in a crafting table
which only allows you to craft the Basic Circuit - and rather inefficiently at that.

## Elite Circuitry

### Basic Logic

<ItemGrid>
<ItemIcon id="fluoropolymer_sheet" />
<ItemIcon id="silver_plate" />
<ItemIcon id="elite_circuit_board" />
<ItemIcon id="logic_core" />
</ItemGrid>

Once access to [Fluoropolymer](polymers.md#fluoropolymer) is secured, silver may be etched onto fluoropolymer sheets
with sulfuric acid to produce high-grade circuit boards which support exotic crystal circuit cores. Logic Cores can
be obtained by fusing [Silicon](elements_base.md#silicon), [Phosphorus](elements_base.md#phosphorus), and [Olivine](gems.md#olivine)
(along with [Argon](elements_base.md#argon) as shielding gas) in the [Arc Furnace](../machines/processing/arc_furnace.md).
Only 1-2 logic cores and a single plate of conductive metal will manufacture Basic, Advanced, and Precision Circuits
in higher quantities and without requiring the previous circuit tier.

### Nano Logic

<ItemGrid>
<ItemIcon id="nano_logic_core" />
<ItemIcon id="t4_circuit" />
<ItemIcon id="t5_circuit" />
</ItemGrid>

Fusing Logic Cores with Pyroxene and [Niobium](metals_alloys.md#niobium) dust will form a Nano Logic Core, the most advanced
computer core created by us. The Nano-Core Processor and Real Virtuality Processor may be created from the Nano Logic Core,
High-Energy Voltaic Cells, titanium shielding plates, and some specialty chemicals.
