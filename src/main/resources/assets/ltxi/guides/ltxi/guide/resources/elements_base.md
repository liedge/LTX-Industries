# Base Elements

Elements are the base building blocks for all chemistry and material production in LTXI. Most, if not all elements are
not naturally found in their base states. Luckily, they can be obtained with minimal processing with our machine lineup.

## Hydrogen

<ItemImage id="portable_tank" components="limacore:fluid={id:'ltxi:hydrogen', amount:16000}" scale="2" />

Hydrogen is used for the mass production of Ammonia and Hydrochloric Acid, as well as for several reduction
reactions to process Tungsten and Rhenium precursors into a more usable state. Additionally, it is used for the impulse
engines of self-propelled projectile weaponry.

Obtained by [ElectroCentrifuging](../machines/processing/electrocentrifuge.md) water. This process is slow and power intensive,
a sturdy power grid and moderate advancement in power supply upgrades is recommended.

## Carbon

<ItemImage id="carbon_dust" />

Obtained by [Grinding](../machines/processing/grinder.md) coal and charcoal. Alloying with iron and crushed deepslate
produces a strong yet easily accessible alloy, Slatesteel.

## Nitrogen

<ItemImage id="portable_tank" components="limacore:fluid={id:'ltxi:nitrogen', amount:16000}" scale="2" />

Nitrogen is the Overworld's atmospheric gas. Inexhaustible and collectible in large amounts, but requires the specialized
[Atmospheric Scrubber](../machines/resource/atmospheric_scrubber.md).

## Oxygen

<ItemImage id="portable_tank" components="limacore:fluid={id:'ltxi:oxygen', amount:16000}" scale="2" />

A uniquely reactive element. It can be produces by ElectroCentrifuging water or silicon dioxide sources. It's primary
application is fusing iron, carbon, and deepslate into Slatesteel. It can also oxidize Sulfur into Sulphurine if
synthesis is preferred over atmospheric extraction.

## Sodium

<ItemImage id="sodium_dust" />

The element of electricity. Obtained from grinding [Spark Fruit](spark_fruits.md). This is the basis for electricity-based
components and upgrades.

## Silicon

<ItemImage id="silicon_dust" />

The backbone of all electronics. It is obtained by ElectroCentrifuging silicon dioxide sources such as sand, flint, or quartz.
Not very useful in its dust form. The [Arc Furnace](../machines/processing/arc_furnace.md) is hot enough to fuse silicon
dust into ingots for use in basic electronics. Once you have access to advanced shielding gases, you can make crystal-phosphorus
composites for advanced electronics.

## Phosphorus

<ItemImage id="phosphorus_dust" />

The element of fire and light. This is the basis for fire-based weaponry, and it is also the base ingredient for
[Neon Lights](../building_blocks/neon_lights.md).

## Sulfur

<ItemImage id="sulfur_dust" />

Sulfur is a vital element for reactive chemistry. It is obtained as a byproduct of grinding coal at a reasonably high
rate. Unfortunately, it is not very useful in its elemental dust state. [Reacting](../machines/processing/chem_lab.md)
it with Oxygen will produce Sulphurine gas.

## Chlorine

<ItemImage id="portable_tank" components="limacore:fluid={id:'ltxi:chlorine', amount:16000}" scale="2" />

Chlorine is a toxic yet invaluable gas used for producing Hydrochloric Acid, Fluoropolymer, and Silicone Oil. Kelp contains
a high amount of bio-available chlorine, enough for the ElectroCentrifuge to extract. Alternatively, seawater
collected by the Atmospheric Scrubber may be ElectroCentrifuged for chlorine.

## Argon

<ItemImage id="portable_tank" components="limacore:fluid={id:'ltxi:argon', amount:16000}" scale="2" />

Argon is the End's atmospheric gas and the key to advanced electronics and metals. It is used by the Arc Furnace
as a shielding gas to produce silicon crystal-phosphorus composites and smelt Tungsten-Plated Slatesteel and Rhenium.
