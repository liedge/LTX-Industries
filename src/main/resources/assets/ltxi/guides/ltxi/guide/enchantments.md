# Enchantments

## Razor

- Compatible with: `#minecraft:enchantable/sharp_weapon`
- Enchantment table/anvil max level: 3

The Razor enchantment primarily serves to collect mob heads from defeated mobs. Certain enemies can drop extra loot.

### Mob Beheading

- 10% drop chance per level for standard heads/skulls.
- **Level 4+ only**: 50% chance per level after 4 to drop an Ender Dragon head.

### Miscellaneous Loot

- 33% drop chance per level for a Rabbit Foot from defeated rabbits.
