# Polymers

As tempting as it is to make everything out of Titanium and other metals, it is not efficient and as technology gets more
complex it becomes outright impossible. Enter the *Polymers*, versatile composite materials made from (mostly) organic
materials. At least, they were organic at some point.

## Polymer

<ItemGrid>
<ItemIcon id="resinous_biomass" />
<ItemIcon id="polymer" />
</ItemGrid>

The first polymer you will make is Polymer, also known as Bio Polymer. It's actually not, but pretend it is so things
don't get confusing. This material is made from [Grinding](../machines/processing/grinder.md) resinous plants,
namely Sugar Cane and Bamboo, to extract Resinous Biomass. The Resinous Biomass can be [Mixed](../machines/processing/mixer.md)
in a bath of Water and [Sulfuric Acid](acids.md#sulfuric-acid) to congeal into polymer bars.

## Fluoropolymer

<ItemImage id="fluoropolymer" />

Fluoropolymer is the advanced version of Polymer. The crafting chain might seem complicated, and it kind of is. Automation
is highly recommended here. Fluoropolymer can be produced by reacting [Hydrofluoric Acid](acids.md#hydrofluoric-acid),
[Methane](elements_compound.md#methane), and [Chlorine](elements_base.md#chlorine) with a non-consumed Titanium Dust
in the [Chem Lab](../machines/processing/chem_lab.md). This will produce Fluoropolymer Bars and some Hydrochloric Acid
as waste.
