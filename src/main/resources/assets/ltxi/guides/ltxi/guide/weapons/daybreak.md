# *Daybreak* 41/RL

<ItemImage id="daybreak" scale="3" />

The *Daybreak* is a heavy rocket launcher specializing in eliminating high threat targets. Upon impact, it will spawn
a swarm of 7 Homing Bomblets. For a direct hit, these will all be targeting the impacted entity (if it's still alive,
that is) or will idle until a valid target wanders into their range. These bomblets can only acquire a target once,
and will only damage their target. If you need widespread destruction, consider the [Hanabi](hanabi.md).

## Stats

- Base direct hit damage: 100.0
- Base splash damage: 40.0
- Base bomblet damage: 15.0
- Projectile: Rockets
- Projectile speed: 30 m/s
- Trigger: Semi Auto
- Trigger cooldown: 1.5 sec
