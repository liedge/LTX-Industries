Despite the name, Neon Lights don't actually contain neon. They do, however, contain [Phosphorus](../resources/elements_base.md#phosphorus)
fused with a dye into a decorative glowing block. Neon Lights can be made in the [Arc Furnace](../machines/processing/arc_furnace.md)
and have a high material to final product yield ratio. The standard 16 dyes can be used, in addition to 5 extra colors
from our custom pigments.
