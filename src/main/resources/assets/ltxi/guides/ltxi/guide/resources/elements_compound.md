# Compound Elements

Some elemental compounds are found or are readily accessible from natural resources. Like base elements, these can be
extracted with some machine processing.

## Methane

<ItemImage id="portable_tank" components="limacore:fluid={id:'ltxi:methane', amount:16000}" scale="2" />

Methane is an abundant hydrocarbon gas found in the Overworld's Swamps. Requires the [Atmospheric Scrubber](../machines/resource/atmospheric_scrubber.md)
to harvest. It is an efficient fuel source, as well as the feedstock for advanced polymers and silicone compounds.

## Sulphurine

<ItemImage id="portable_tank" components="limacore:fluid={id:'ltxi:sulphurine', amount:16000}" scale="2" />

Sulphurine is the Nether's atmospheric gas. The otherwise impossibly dry conditions of the Nether allow this gas to remain
stable and not decay to elemental sulfur. The [Chem Lab](../machines/processing/chem_lab.md) can react Sulphurine
with Water to form Sulfuric Acid, and it is also used to solidify Silicone Oil into rubber.

## Seawater

<ItemImage id="portable_tank" components="limacore:fluid={id:'ltxi:sea_water', amount:16000}" scale="2" />

Water from the Overworld's oceans has a high concentration of chlorine from marine flora. An Atmospheric Scrubber can
harvest the ocean's water, and Chlorine may subsequently be extracted via [ElectroCentrifuge](../machines/processing/electrocentrifuge.md).

## Ammonia

<ItemImage id="portable_tank" components="limacore:fluid={id:'ltxi:ammonia', amount:16000}" scale="2" />

Ammonia is a toxic, nitrogen-hydrogen compound used to grow certain exotic flora in the [Bio/ARU Garden](../machines/resource/digital_garden.md)
and for gloom gas weaponry. More importantly, it is used in the processing line for Rhenium metal.
