Peridotite is a beautiful green rock that is commonly found underground like Andesite, Granite, and Diorite. You can
craft it into its Polished variant and make stairs, slabs, and walls. 

## Peridotite Dust

<ItemImage id="peridotite_dust" />

Peridotite serves as an important ingredient for making advanced technology. [Grinding](../machines/processing/grinder.md)
Peridotite into dust and then processing mass amounts of it through a [HydroSieve](../machines/processing/hydrosieve.md)
produces raw [Olivine](../resources/gems.md#olivine).
