# Acids

## Sulfuric Acid

<ItemImage id="portable_tank" components="limacore:fluid={id:'ltxi:sulfuric_acid', amount:16000}" scale="2" />

Sulfuric Acid is the basis of polymer chemistry, circuit etching, and organic material dissolution. It is also used
by the [fourth stage](../ore_processing.md#stage-4) of the ore processing chain. This acid may be produced by
[Mixing](../machines/processing/mixer.md) the [crushed pulp](vitriol_berries.md#acidic-biomass) of Vitriol Berries and
Water. Alternatively, [Sulphurine](elements_compound.md#sulphurine) may undergo a controlled but slow reaction
with Water in the [Chem Lab](../machines/processing/chem_lab.md) to yield Sulfuric Acid.

## Hydrochloric Acid

<ItemImage id="portable_tank" components="limacore:fluid={id:'ltxi:hydrochloric_acid', amount:16000}" scale="2" />

Hydrochloric Acid is a purifying acid used by the [fifth stage](../ore_processing.md#stage-5) of the ore processing chain.
As the name might suggest, it is made by mixing an equal ratio of Hydrogen and Chlorine in the Chem Lab. It is also
produced as a waste product from Fluoropolymer and Silicone Oil manufacturing, though this should only be considered
a supplementary source.

## Hydrofluoric Acid

<ItemImage id="portable_tank" components="limacore:fluid={id:'ltxi:hydrofluoric_acid', amount:16000}" scale="2" />

Hydrofluoric Acid is highly toxic, but it is one of the three key ingredients for producing Fluoropolymer. It can be
made by dissolving [Fluorite](gems.md#fluorite) gems in Sulfuric Acid in the Chem Lab.
