# Wonderland Armor

The AL/1C powered modular exosuit, codename '*Wonderland*', is LTXI's signature armor set. It can be [upgraded](fundamentals/upgrade_system.md)
to increase its defense, utility, protection against harsh environments, and energy storage.

All AL/1C pieces are [Fabricated](machines/processing/fabricators.md) with 2 standard-issue defense upgrades and an intrinsic
upgrade unique to the piece. The defense upgrade modules are entry level and can be upgraded to heavily increase the
armor's defense.
