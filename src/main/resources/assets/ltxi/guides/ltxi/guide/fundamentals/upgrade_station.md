# Upgrade Station

<BlockImage id="upgrade_station" scale="4" />

The Upgrade Station is your one-stop shop for tinkering with your ε-series or signature series equipment. Insert a valid
equipment item, and then you can manage its Upgrades and Light Colors.

## Light Colors

You can change an equipment's Primary (P), Secondary (S), or Energy (E) light channel color to any RGB value. That is,
if the equipment supports said channel. If you don't see it when you open this sub-screen, then the equipment item
doesn't support changing it.

> All color changes must be applied to take effect!
