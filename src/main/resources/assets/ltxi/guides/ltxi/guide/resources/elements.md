# Elements

## Carbon

<ItemImage id="carbon_dust" />

Obtained by grinding charcoal and coals.

## Sodium

<ItemImage id="sodium_dust" />

The element of electricity. Obtained from grinding [Spark Fruit](spark_fruits.md). Used for electricity-based components,
voltaic cells, and other energy manufacturing.

## Silicon

<ItemImage id="silicon_dust" />

The backbone of all circuitry. Not very useful in its dust state. Can be smelted into ingots in the Arc Furnace and then
pressed into plates for basic circuitry.

## Phosphorus

<ItemImage id="phosphorus_dust" />

The element of fire and light. Useful for fire-based weaponry, neon lights, and for making advanced silicon composites
for the efficient manufacturing of circuits.

## Sulfur

<ItemImage id="sulfur_dust" />

The precursor for Sulfuric Acid, a key ingredient in polymer and circuitry production. Must be oxidized into Sulphurine
to be useful.
